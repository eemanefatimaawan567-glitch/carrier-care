    Pilot proposal

    A plan to test the idea safely. Not a deployed system.

    Phase 1 · Months 1–3

    Agree scope with a health authority and clinical advisors. Clinical review of all content. Privacy and security assessment.

    Phase 2 · Months 4–8

    Small pilot at screening sites, with consent. Measure comprehension, counseling uptake, time to referral and privacy incidents.

    Phase 3 · Months 9–12

    Review results with clinicians. Decide together whether to widen the pilot.

    What we would measure

    Understanding of results across languages and literacy levels. Counseling uptake. Time to referral. Privacy incidents (target: zero). Never: cancelled marriages or any single personal choice.

    All targets to be set with clinical partners
