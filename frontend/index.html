LEARN WITHOUT PRESSURE

A few important things to know.

What is a carrier?

A carrier has a genetic change associated with an inherited condition. For many recessive conditions, a carrier usually has no symptoms, though there are exceptions.

Is 25% a diagnosis?

No. For an autosomal recessive condition when both parents carry disease-causing variants in the same gene, 25% is a probability for each pregnancy — not a diagnosis for a particular child.

Can someone decline sharing?

Yes. In this demonstration, either person can decline and the shared result remains hidden. Real deployments require secure authentication and independently verified access controls.

What if a finding is uncertain?

An uncertain variant should not be treated as a confirmed disease-causing result. Qualified genetics professionals can consider the clinical context and whether further assessment is useful.
