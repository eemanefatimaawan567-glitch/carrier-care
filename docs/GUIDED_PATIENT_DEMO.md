# Amanah patient-friendly guided demo

Open **frontend/index.html** and click **Start the guided demo**. Four steps: (1) choose synthetic scenario, (2) two optional independent consents, (3) plain-language result or locked state, (4) next steps, counselor question download, and an educational comprehension check.

Both consent checkboxes start **unchecked**. This guide is a client-only teaching illustration, not the backend privacy engine. It does not accept or upload personal medical data, and the question download contains only fixed general questions.

Language buttons switch this guided section between English and Arabic, including the fixed result messages. Other technical sections may retain their original language support. The demonstration offers **no real appointment scheduling**, certified clinician, clinical endorsement, or personalized risk analysis. The 1-in-4 explanation applies only to the specified autosomal recessive example when both partners carry disease-causing variants for the same condition, and is not based on relatedness alone.

Suggested demo recording: choose 'Both people carry a change'; leave one consent unchecked and show locked result; go back and check both; show probability and limitations; advance to questions and quiz; switch to Arabic; then show technical backend controls separately.
