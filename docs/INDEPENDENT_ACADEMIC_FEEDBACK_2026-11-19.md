## Feedback received (faithful paraphrase, not direct quotation)

1. **Inheritance risk:** The classic autosomal recessive 25% / 50% / 25% calculation applies to parents carrying pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in the same gene causing the same condition; variants need not be identical. Each pregnancy is independent. A 25% probability of inheriting both variants does not always equal 25% clinically affected when penetrance varies. A negative limited screen retains residual risk.
2. **Referral:** Relevant triggers include ongoing pregnancy (especially known carrier plus pending/unavailable partner result), X-linked or health-relevant findings, conflicting interpretations/VUS, a known diagnosis or familial variant, prior affected pregnancy/child, recurrent pregnancy loss, infant deaths, congenital/developmental history and relatedness.
3. **VUS:** Uncertain classification is not confirmed carrier status; do not use a VUS as a positive match or basis for reproductive guidance; consider counseling and possible reinterpretation.
4. **Consent:** Each person must explicitly authorize a comparison and sharing; a shared-match alert itself can reveal genetic information and must respect the same consent boundary.
5. **Counselor summary:** For a future clinically governed referral, include pregnancy status/timing, authorized lab/report details, relevant family and reproductive history, questions requiring counseling, consent scope and secure contact method. The current fictional educational handoff is not a complete clinical intake.
