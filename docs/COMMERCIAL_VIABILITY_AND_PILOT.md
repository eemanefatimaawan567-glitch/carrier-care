# Amanah Genomics — commercial viability and pilot pathway

Status: **hypothesis / pre-commercial**, prepared for the Future Health Digital Innovation Challenge.

## Customer and payer

Users: individuals learning about carrier screening, using free educational access. Prospective purchasers: screening centres, genetic counseling providers, hospitals and public health programs. All are customer hypotheses; no deals or institutional commitments exist.

## Product differentiation

Consent-first patient education with English/Arabic support, risk explanations, inheritance graphics, educational quizzes, counseling preparation and auditable sharing concepts. Complements testing and counseling; does not replace clinicians or clinical screening laboratories.

## Pricing hypothesis

Proposed organizational subscription: AED 750 per facility per month, to test in customer interviews. This is **not** a researched market price or an agreed offer.

Illustrative 5-facility scenario: monthly gross revenue AED 3,750; annual gross revenue AED 45,000; assumed per-facility variable cost AED 250/month; contribution before fixed costs AED 30,000/year. All values are planning assumptions, not earned revenue. Excludes clinical/regulatory work, security assurance, integrations, sales, engineering and overhead.

## Validation plan

1. Independent qualified clinical content and Arabic language review. Asif Jamal consented to attribution for checking **five specific statements** only; this is not clinical validation or a whole-product endorsement.
2. Consent-based usability study with safe fictional cases: task completion, comprehension pre/post, time to find counseling information, bilingual parity and perceived clarity; record sample size, recruitment and limitations.
3. Seek permission for supervised provider pilot using synthetic or appropriately de-identified data, with privacy, legal and clinical approvals before real clinical integration.
4. Obtain buyer feedback on proposed pricing, procurement, integration costs, clinician workload and ongoing support.

## Pilot success criteria (proposed, not results)

- Task success rates and comprehension improvements reported transparently with sample size.
- No unauthorized sharing in specified consent-gate test cases.
- Comparable accessibility and comprehension evaluations across Arabic and English.
- Counselor feedback on usefulness, including limitations and error cases.
- Documented staffing, security, hosting and compliance costs before institutional pricing.

## Non-claims

No paying clients, signed pilots, government endorsement, formal clinical validation, certified medical device status, or UAE Pass/Malaffi integration is claimed. Do not enter real genetic data in the demo.
