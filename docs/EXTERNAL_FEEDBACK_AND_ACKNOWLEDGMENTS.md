## Asif Jamal — limited factual / clarity review (October 20, 2026)

Asif Jamal responded to a brief set of five genetics statements with the responses **1Y, 2Y, 3Y, 4Y, 5Y**. He gave permission to acknowledge his contribution.

The topics checked were:
1. People who carry one pathogenic variant for a typical autosomal recessive disorder are often asymptomatic.
2. For two carriers of the same typical autosomal recessive condition, each pregnancy has a **25% affected / 50% unaffected carrier / 25% unaffected non-carrier** Mendelian example, subject to the variant and condition context.
3. Consanguinity changes the chance that both partners share an inherited variant; it does **not** change the classic 25% Mendelian chance *once both parents are established carriers of the same autosomal recessive condition*.
4. Variants of uncertain significance should not be used to assign a definitive reproductive-risk figure; qualified interpretation is needed.
5. Negative and shared-carrier findings should be explained clearly and non-directively, with limits of screening stated.

**Boundary:** This was a brief fact/wording check, not a full app, code, Arabic translation, or clinical workflow assessment. No endorsement or formal clinical validation is claimed. Qualifications and practice scope are not independently verified here.
